Data Deletion Policy

Your data rights and how to request deletion

Users may request deletion of their account information and associated data by contacting Techorses.

How to Request Deletion

Deletion requests can be submitted through:

Email: hey@techorses.com

Required Information

Please include the following details in your deletion request:

  • Your name - Full name as registered
  • Registered email - Email address used for the account
  • Business/account information - Any relevant account details
  • Details of the deletion request - Specific information about what data you want deleted

Processing Timeline

We will process eligible deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal, operational, and compliance obligations.

Note: Some data may need to be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes even after a deletion request.

What Gets Deleted

Upon verification of your deletion request, we will remove:

  • Personal account information
  • Communication history
  • Usage data and analytics
  • Uploaded content associated with your account

Data that may be retained: Anonymized data, transaction records for legal compliance, and security logs as required by law.
