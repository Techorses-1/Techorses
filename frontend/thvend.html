About TH Vend

TH Vend is a powerful retail management system that brings complete clarity, control, and confidence to your buying and selling process.

Instead of juggling files, spreadsheets, and paper bills, TH Vend puts your entire retail workflow into one simple, easy-to-use platform.

It helps you track what you purchase, what you sell, and what's in stock - all in real time. Whether it's supplier orders, customer purchases, or inventory levels, you always know what's happening, without searching or guessing.
